Dr. Cacciatore is a Professor at Arizona State University, founder of the MISS Foundation, and an internationally recognized scholar whose groundbreaking work on traumatic grief, compassionate care, and bereavement has influenced research and practice around the world. Together, they'll explore some questions that I think we all need to be asking, such as: Can we truly heal without addressing the social and political conditions that produce suffering?, How do institutions and systems of power shape whose grief is recognized, whose suffering is dismissed, and whose healing is supported?, What would it mean to approach healing through compassion, collective care, and justice rather than simply asking individuals to adapt to harmful conditions? This is exactly why we're building this series! We want to create spaces where we can think critically, challenge assumptions, learn from one another, and imagine what social work could become when we take our commitments to justice seriously. **And these conversations are for EVERYONE!** Students, educators, practitioners, organizers, and community members are all welcome.
