We’re excited to welcome [Payment for Placements (P4P) - U.S.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/payment-for-placements/) as a co-sponsor of NCRJI-SW's National Open Letter to CSWE!

Together, we're advocating for a social work profession that takes racial justice, economic justice, and anti-oppressive education seriously.

Our next goal is **1,865 signatures**, honoring the abolition of slavery in 1865 and reminding us that the struggle for racial justice continues.

Then we're heading toward 1,994 and 2,022—milestones honoring the origins of reproductive justice and the explicit anti-racist commitments of the 2022 EPAS.

**Help us reach 1,865!**

Sign here: [https://lnkd.in/eDifWnKu](https://lnkd.in/eDifWnKu)

Public and confidential support are both welcome.

Please share the flyer with your networks and encourage others to sign!
